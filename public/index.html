The substitution problem

Same package code. Different fit.

Replacement parts can share a package code and still route signals differently or exceed what the board allows. A mistaken substitution can delay a release or trigger a redesign.

Pin functionsThe same position can carry a different signal.
Footprint and clearancePads, mask and enclosure geometry affect the design.
Board revisionBind every finding to the exact design.

Questions

What to know before an evaluation.

Start with a candidate and a specific engineering decision.

Where does the candidate come from?

Use your current cross-reference, distributor or procurement process. You supply the candidates for the pilot.

What can the public demo do?

Inspect fictional sources, record a simulated rejection or leave a case open, and export a fictional record.

Who signs off on a real part?

Your authorized engineer owns qualification and release. Your library owner controls changes to released libraries.

What if evidence is incomplete?

The review names the missing item, its owner and next action. The case can remain open.

What files should we prepare?

Licensed datasheets, authorized board and library revisions, and prior decisions in one component family. Agree how to exchange them before sharing.

How much does the evaluation cost?

The evaluation is scoped and quoted during discovery. The proposed annual team license is $60,000 with part-library tiers.
